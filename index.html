Why Kanpai

We’ve never been more connected, or more alone.

About half of U.S. adults say they’ve felt lonely, and in 2020 Americans spent around 20 minutes a day with friends in person, down from an hour two decades earlier (U.S. Surgeon General, 2023). The fix isn’t another feed. It’s a room, a table and someone who bothered to host. Hosts carry those nights mostly out of their own pockets. Kanpai makes it easy for the brands and people who care to keep them going.
